description=Fetch NBR from NASA HLS imagery, classify dNBR, RdNBR or RBR into burn severity classes, and summarize the area of each class for a fire and its sub-areas.
about=Fire Analysis Processing tools. Imagery: find NASA HLS v2.0 (Landsat 8/9 and Sentinel-2) scenes over an area of interest and fetch cloud-masked NBR for just that window (needs a NASA Earthdata login in ~/.netrc). Severity: Burn severity summary builds a burned-area zone and optional sub-area zones such as watersheds, classifies raw dNBR with the USGS Key & Benson (2006) thresholds or RdNBR (Miller & Thode 2007) and RBR (Parks et al. 2014) from a pre-fire NBR raster, counts pixels per class with Zonal Histogram, and writes a one-row-per-class summary table (percent, hectares and acres for each zone) ready for a layout bar chart. Uses only QGIS's built-in Processing tools.
